Seatery current update — September 22, 2026

This package includes:
- Up to 32 chairs per table
- Rectangular tables with no end chairs
- Rectangular tables with one chair at each end
- Custom rectangular layouts with draggable chairs
- Rectangular tables grow with chair count, with leader lines from chairs to names
- Guest-list CSV export includes food choice, table number, description, and seat number
- Guests have editable food choices and tables have editable descriptions
- Guests have a separate dietary-restrictions field and export column
- Save project and Export PNG remain visible; other actions are in the More menu
- Existing guest editing/deletion, table dragging, and complete PNG export

To verify the update, open a rectangular table and look for the "Chair placement"
selector in the Edit table dialog.
